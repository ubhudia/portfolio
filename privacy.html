Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data I collect, why I collect it, and what you can do about it. I am Umesh Bhudia, a fractional and interim Chief Marketing Officer working as a sole trader in Wembley, London.

For the purposes of UK data protection law I am the data controller for the information described here. I am registered in England.

The short version. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no tracking scripts, and has no forms. I only hold your personal data if you contact me or book a call, and then only to reply and to do the work. I do not sell or share it for marketing.

1. This website does not track you

The site is a set of static pages hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It contains no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels, no session recording and no browser storage of any kind. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Cloudflare, as the host, automatically records technical information such as your IP address and browser type in its server logs, in the same way any web server does. I have no access to those logs and cannot identify individual visitors. Cloudflare processes that data under its own privacy statement.

Links away from this site

Buttons marked as booking a call open Cal.com. Links to LinkedIn open LinkedIn. Those are separate services with their own privacy policies and their own cookies. This policy does not cover them.

3. Client data during an engagement

Fractional CMO work means working inside your systems. During an engagement I may be given access to a CRM, a marketing automation platform, an analytics account or a data warehouse that contains personal data about your customers, candidates or prospects.

In that situation you are the data controller and I act as a processor on your behalf. That relationship is governed by the contract or data processing agreement we sign, not by this policy. As a matter of practice:

  • I access only the systems and records needed for the work.
  • I do not export or retain your customer data beyond what an engagement requires.
  • Access is returned or revoked when the engagement ends.
  • Anything I learn about your business is treated as confidential.

4. Case studies and references

The results published on this site describe work delivered in roles at named employers and clients. They report business outcomes such as pipeline, conversion and cost metrics. They contain no personal data about any individual, and no confidential customer or candidate records.

If you are a former colleague or client and would prefer a specific reference removed, email me and I will remove it.

5. Who else sees your data

I use a small number of service providers to run the business. Each processes data on my instructions:

  • Cal.com for scheduling
  • Cloudflare Pages for hosting this site
  • Google Workspace for email, calendar and file storage
  • An accountant, for invoices and statutory records

I do not sell your personal data, and I do not share it for anyone else's marketing. I will disclose information if the law requires it.

Some of these providers operate outside the UK. Where data is transferred internationally it is protected by UK adequacy regulations or by standard contractual clauses.

7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask me to:

  • tell you what personal data I hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete it, where I have no continuing reason to keep it
  • restrict how I use it, or object to my using it
  • provide it in a portable format
  • withdraw consent, where consent was the basis for holding it

Email umesh.bhudia@gmail.com and I will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Keeping it safe

Accounts are protected with strong unique passwords and multi-factor authentication. Devices are encrypted. Access to client systems uses named accounts rather than shared credentials, so it can be audited and revoked. I keep the number of places your data sits as small as the work allows.

No system is completely secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, I will notify you and the ICO within the timescales the law requires.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change materially affects how I handle your data, I will tell affected clients directly.
